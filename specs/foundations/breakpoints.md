# Breakpoints

The page grid changes at two widths. Both land on the 8px scale.

| Name | Min width | Columns | Page margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Narrow | 0 | 4 | `--space-16` (16px) |
| Medium | 48rem (768px) | 8 | `--space-24` (24px) |
| Wide | 64rem (1024px) | 12 | `--space-32` (32px) |

The gutter is `--space-24` at every width. Columns, margins, and the collapse order are specified in `patterns/responsive-grid.md`.
